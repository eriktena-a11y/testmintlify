## March 2026

### Modes

Create custom modes that teach Hazel how you work. We built Modes so advisors can save their instructions, templates, and preferences for repeated chats once — and have them one click away in any new chat. Think of it like creating your own personalized instructions for estate planning, financial planning, retirement, and more. Build a **Firm Coach mode** so advisors can tap into firm knowledge instantly or an **Email mode** that turns any rough draft into a polished email using the right style guide. See [overview](https://docs.hazel.ai/guides/modes/overview) or visit our [Modes prompt library](https://docs.hazel.ai/resources/prompt-library/modes) to see how other advisors are taking advantage.

### **Tasks**

You can now create, edit, and delete tasks from a dedicated Task view, or ask Hazel to create one for you in chat. For example: _"Hey Hazel, create a high priority task due tomorrow to complete the ACAT for The Smith Family." _This view is great for advisors that see a surge of clients per day or for firm managers that want a way to see what's been assigned and what's still pending for their team at a glance.

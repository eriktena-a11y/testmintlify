## June 2026

### New features

#### Custom Modes

Build your own Modes in Ask Hazel. Package your instructions, tone, and reference files (PDF, DOCX, XLSX, TXT, CSV) into a reusable Mode, then start a focused chat in one click. Keep a Mode private or share it firm-wide so the whole team works from the same playbook. See [Custom Modes](/guides/modes/custom-modes) or browse the [Modes prompt library](/resources/prompt-library/modes) for ready-made instructions.

#### HubSpot integration

Connect HubSpot to Hazel for a bidirectional sync of contacts, households, tasks, and notes. Updates flow instantly from Hazel to HubSpot, and HubSpot syncs back every 60 minutes—including a full historical backfill on first connect. See [HubSpot](/guides/integrations/crms/hub-spot).

#### Team management: Groups and automatic rules

Manage who on your team can see which clients without sharing one record at a time. Create **Groups** (service teams, pods, etc.) and share households or contacts with the whole Group at once. Add **automatic rules** to share clients that match by **Owner** or **Tag**—including any future clients that match. See [Managing Teams](/guides/settings/members/managing-teams).

### Updates

- **Firm Admin permission.** Firm Admin is now a separate permission you can grant an Advisor so they can manage the team and billing without changing which clients they can see. See [Roles and Permissions](/guides/settings/members/roles-and-permissions).
- **Per-client access levels.** When sharing a household or contact, choose **Read**, **Write**, or **Manage** to control how teammates can interact with it.
- **Outlook support clarified.** The Outlook integration supports Microsoft Office 365 and Microsoft Enterprise. Microsoft Exchange on-premise is not supported. See [Outlook](/guides/integrations/calendar-and-emails/outlook).
- **Billing for Admin AI + Tax Planning.** Admin AI is now the base seat, with Tax Planning billed per seat as an add-on. Owners and Admins can manage subscriptions in **Settings → Billing**. See [Billing](/guides/settings/billing).

## March 2026

### Modes

Create custom Modes that teach Hazel how you work. Set up your instructions, templates, and preferences once — then have them one click away in any new chat. Build a **Firm Coach Mode** so advisors can tap into firm best practices instantly or an **Estate Planning Specialist** **Mode** that produces a summary, asset distribution by scenario, and recommendations the moment you upload an estate plan. See the [overview](https://docs.hazel.ai/guides/modes/overview) or visit our [Modes prompt library](https://docs.hazel.ai/resources/prompt-library/modes) to see how other advisors are putting Modes to work.

### **Tasks**

You can now create, edit, and delete tasks from a dedicated Task view, or ask Hazel to create one for you in chat. For example: "Hey Hazel, create a high priority task due tomorrow to complete the ACAT for The Smith Family." This view is great for advisors that see a surge of clients per day or for firm managers that want a way to see what's been assigned and what's still pending for their team at a glance.

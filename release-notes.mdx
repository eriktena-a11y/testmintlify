## June 2026

### New features

#### Team management: Groups and automatic rules

Manage who on your team can see which clients without sharing one record at a time. Create **Groups** and share households or contacts with the whole Group at once, then add **automatic rules** to share clients that match by **Owner** or **Tag**—including any future clients that match. See [Managing Teams](/guides/settings/members/managing-teams).

#### Performance Dashboard for firm owners

Firm owners get a new **Performance Dashboard** that rolls up meeting metrics across the firm. Filter by meeting type, timeframe, and advisor to see firm-wide averages, per-advisor breakdowns, and trends—then drill into any cell to the underlying meetings. See [Performance Dashboard](/guides/meeting-assistant/meeting-details/performance-dashboard).

## May 2026

### New features

#### Improvements to in-person meetings

Improvements to how the audio is captured, separated by speaker, and flows into the same notes, tasks, and analysis as your virtual meetings. See [Recording a meeting](/guides/meeting-assistant/recording-a-meeting).

#### Improvements to meeting agendas and pre-meeting prep

Hazel's meeting agendas are now more powerful—pulling in relevant context about the household, including account details from your custodian—and you can run pre-meeting prep with one click. See [Preparing for a meeting](/guides/meeting-assistant/preparing-for-a-meeting).

#### File generation and polished reports available in chat

We added file generation to Modes and Ask Hazel, so Hazel can produce downloadable files—like PDF reports—right from a Mode or a chat. See [Custom Modes](/guides/modes/custom-modes). Ask Hazel for a report—a tax analysis, a plan summary—and get back a clean, consistently formatted PDF with a uniform layout, charts, and disclaimers, ready to download right from the chat. See [Ask Hazel](/guides/ask-hazel-chat/overview).

#### See where every tax number comes from

Tax Mode dashboard cards now have a toggle that reveals the source and derivation of each figure—brackets, deductions, MAGI, Medicare, and more—so you can show clients exactly how a value was calculated. See [Tax Planning](/guides/modes/tax-mode).

### Minor improvements

- **iPhone photos accepted.** HEIC and HEIF images convert automatically on upload, so you can attach iPhone photos without a format error.
- **Tasks filters that stick.** The Tasks view now remembers your filters between visits and lets you apply several at once.
- **More reliable meeting recording.** Fewer duplicate or missed notetaker bots, and steadier post-meeting processing.

## April 2026

### New features

#### HubSpot integration

Connect HubSpot to Hazel for a bidirectional sync of contacts, households, tasks, and notes. Updates flow instantly from Hazel to HubSpot, and HubSpot syncs back every 60 minutes—including a full historical backfill on first connect. See [HubSpot](/guides/integrations/crms/hub-spot).

#### Redesigned Meeting Details

A reworked meeting page brings the summary, agenda, and full transcript into one feed, with attendees up top, a cleaner tasks panel, and a one-click button to sync notes to your CRM. See [Recording a meeting](/guides/meeting-assistant/recording-a-meeting).

#### Rich, interactive answers in chat

Hazel can now answer with formatted, interactive components—tables, bar/line/pie charts, metric cards, and callouts—so the numbers and comparisons you ask for come back ready to read at a glance. See [Ask Hazel](/guides/ask-hazel-chat/overview).

#### Sync a tax analysis to Salesforce

When Hazel produces a tax analysis, push it into Salesforce with a single button—Hazel adds a note on the household and attaches the analysis PDF under the household's Files. Requires a connected Salesforce CRM. See [Tax Planning](/guides/modes/tax-mode).

#### Compliance archiving with Hadrius

Connect Hadrius from Settings to automatically archive your recorded meetings and transcripts for compliance, delivered after each meeting is analyzed. See [Integration settings](/guides/settings/integration-settings).

### Minor improvements

- **Sharper meeting notes and transcripts.** Upgraded language and speech models make summaries read more naturally and in-person transcripts more accurate.
- **Image documents supported in Tax Mode.** Drop in a photo or scan of a tax document and Hazel reads its figures into your analysis.
- **Filter meetings by household.** The meetings list now has a household filter and shows participants as a column.
- **Faster email lists.** Email views load noticeably faster and flag which messages have attachments.

## March 2026

### Modes

Create custom Modes that teach Hazel how you work. Set up your instructions, templates, and preferences once — then have them one click away in any new chat. Build a **Firm Coach Mode** so advisors can tap into firm best practices instantly or an **Estate Planning Specialist** **Mode** that produces a summary, asset distribution by scenario, and recommendations the moment you upload an estate plan. See the [overview](https://docs.hazel.ai/guides/modes/overview) or visit our [Modes prompt library](https://docs.hazel.ai/resources/prompt-library/modes) to see how other advisors are putting Modes to work.

### **Tasks**

You can now create, edit, and delete tasks from a dedicated Task view, or ask Hazel to create one for you in chat. For example: "Hey Hazel, create a high priority task due tomorrow to complete the ACAT for The Smith Family." This view is great for advisors that see a surge of clients per day or for firm managers that want a way to see what's been assigned and what's still pending for their team at a glance.

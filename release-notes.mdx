## March 2026

### Modes

Create custom Modes that teach Hazel how you work. Set up your instructions, templates, and preferences once — then have them one click away in any new chat. Build a **Firm Coach Mode** so you can tap into firm knowledge instantly or build an **Estate Planning Specialist** **Mode** that produces a summary, asset distribution by scenario, and recommendations the moment you upload an estate plan. See the [overview](https://docs.hazel.ai/guides/modes/overview) or visit our [Modes prompt library](https://docs.hazel.ai/resources/prompt-library/modes) to see how other advisors are putting Modes to work.

### **Tasks**

You can now create, edit, and delete tasks from a dedicated Task view, or ask Hazel to create one for you in chat. For example: \_"Hey Hazel, create a high priority task due tomorrow to complete the ACAT for The Smith Family." \_This view is great for advisors that see a surge of clients per day or for firm managers that want a way to see what's been assigned and what's still pending for their team at a glance.

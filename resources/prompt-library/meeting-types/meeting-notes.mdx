<Note>
  You don’t need to ask for timestamps in your prompt. Hazel automatically cites the moments notes came from with inline `(MM:SS)` markers, and those markers are clickable in the meeting view — they jump the recording to the exact spot the note references. This works with any custom Meeting Notes prompt you write.
</Note>

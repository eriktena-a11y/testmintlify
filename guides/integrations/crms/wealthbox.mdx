title: "Wealthbox"
---

Connect your Wealthbox account to give Hazel access to your contacts, households, tasks, and notes. With a bidirectional sync, updates made in Hazel appear in your CRM, and changes in your CRM are reflected back in Hazel, keeping your data consistent and up to date across both platforms.

<Note>
  Hazel does not import existing roles and permissions from your CRM. It is up to the Owner to edit household permissions for the organization. Learn more about editing household permissions.
</Note>

| **Wealthbox** | Full history | Every hour (incremental updates) | Active contacts only; your firm's sync selection and rules apply (see below) | ✅ Yes |

The Wealthbox integration provides a bi-directional sync for: Tasks, Notes, Contacts, and Households. Hazel instantaneously syncs data to Wealthbox, and Wealthbox syncs data every 60 minutes to Hazel. Upon the initial sync, Hazel aims to provide a complete backfill of historical data.

### Which contacts come into Hazel

Active contacts are eligible by default, whatever their Contact Type or Tags. Hazel doesn't import only Clients and Prospects.

When you set up the connection, you choose which existing contacts to sync. For contacts created in Wealthbox after setup, a firm owner can set rules by **Contact Type** and **Tag**:

- Each rule layer can include or exclude values. Values within a layer match if any of them apply, and every layer must match.
- "No contact type" covers contacts with a blank Contact Type.
- These rules only decide which new contacts are imported automatically. They never remove existing contacts you selected.

To review or change your firm's selection and rules, go to **Settings → Integrations**, open the menu on the Wealthbox card, and choose **Edit sync selection**.

### What doesn't sync

- **Wealthbox Events.** Hazel syncs Wealthbox Notes, not Events. If your history is stored as Events (for example, records pushed in by another notetaker), it won't come into Hazel. The only option today is to recreate the important ones as notes.

### If you disconnect Wealthbox

Everything already synced into Hazel stays in Hazel. New information from that point on isn't synced, so you'll need to update it in Hazel yourself.

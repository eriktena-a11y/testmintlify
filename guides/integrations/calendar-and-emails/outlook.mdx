---
title: "Outlook"
---

Connect your Outlook account to give Hazel access to your calendar events, emails, and the ability to draft responses to your contacts.

<Note>
  Hazel does not send email on your behalf. Every email draft that Hazel creates requires a human in the loop to review prior to sending
</Note>

---

### **Supported Instances**

<Check>
  Microsoft Office 365

  Microsoft Enterprise
</Check>

<Danger>
  Microsoft Exchange

  Hazel does not support Microsoft Exchange on premise today as it requires custom configuration and setup per-firm.
</Danger>

---

### **Email**

Emails from your clients appear on each household's **Emails** tab. Hazel also uses these emails as context to help you prepare for meetings or leverage in Ask Hazel.

To draft a reply, ask Hazel in chat. Drafts are created in Hazel Chat for you to review and send; they aren't placed in your Outlook drafts folder.

You can connect one email account per user.

### Calendar

Hazel views your calendar and automatically scheduled note takers to join. Hazel reviews past and future events and provides you with agendas for each event.

### Permissions to review and accept:

<Note>
  - View events on all your calendars
  - Read, compose, and send emails from your Outlook account
  - See your primary Outlook Account email address
  - Associate you with your personal info on Outlook
</Note>

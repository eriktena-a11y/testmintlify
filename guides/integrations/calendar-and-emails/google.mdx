title: "Google"
---

Connect your Google account to give Hazel access to your calendar events, emails, and the ability to draft responses to your contacts.

<Note>
  Hazel does not send email on your behalf. Every email draft that Hazel creates requires a human in the loop to review prior to sending
</Note>

---

### **Email**

Emails from your clients appear on each household's **Emails** tab. Hazel also uses these emails as context to help you prepare for meetings or leverage in Ask Hazel.

Hazel doesn't draft replies to incoming emails on its own. To draft a reply, ask Hazel in chat, then send it or choose **Save to drafts** to put it in your Gmail drafts folder. With **Auto-save Follow-up Email Drafts** turned on in **Settings → Meetings**, meeting follow-up emails are saved to your Gmail drafts automatically.

Connect Google in **Settings → Profile** under **Email and Calendar**. You can connect one Google account; connecting a different one replaces it.

### Calendar

Hazel views your calendar and automatically scheduled note takers to join. Hazel reviews past and future events and provides you with agendas for each event.

![Googleintegration](/images/googleintegration.gif)

### Permissions to review and accept:

<Note>
  - View events on all your calendars
  - Read, compose, and send emails from your Google account
  - See your primary Google Account email address
  - Associate you with your personal info on Google
</Note>

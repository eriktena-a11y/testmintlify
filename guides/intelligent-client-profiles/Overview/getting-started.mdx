## Before you start

dIntelligent Client Profiles build themselves from your everyday work; there's nothing to configure. Two things determine how quickly they fill in:

1. **Your sources of client information.** Profiles learn from meetings recorded by the [Hazel notetaker](/guides/meeting-assistant/overview/how-it-works), email and CRM connected in **Settings → Integrations**, client documents you upload, and the notes you write in Hazel. Any one of them works; the more sources, the richer the profile.
2. **Your clients in Hazel.** Facts are filed under your Households and Contacts, so the people you meet with need to exist as contacts (Households and Contacts typically sync from a connected CRM, but you can also create them in Hazel).

## Finding the profile

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a household or person">
    Click **Contacts** in the sidebar, pick a household from the **Households** list (or a person under **People**), and open them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look at the tab bar">
    A household page shows **Summary · Meetings · Tasks · Emails · Notes · Documents · Data**. The profile lives across three of these: **Summary** (the narrative view), **Documents** (files Hazel has read), and **Data** (every fact, with sources). Individual client pages have Documents and Data tabs, but no Summary tab.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  A useful way to think about the three: **Summary** is where you _read_ the profile, **Documents** is where you _feed_ it, and **Data** is where you _maintain_ it.
</Tip>

## The Summary tab: your household at a glance

The Summary tab is the profile turned into prose, designed to get you current on a household in about a minute.

- **Summary** is a narrative Hazel writes from the profile. Underlined phrases are cited: click one to see exactly which meeting, email, or document the statement came from. The narrative refreshes itself as new facts arrive.
- **Tasks** and **Meetings** show the household's open work and recent meetings, in place.
- **About Household** covers key dates, a **Goals** card (each goal as it was recorded), and a card for each member with a short bio Hazel maintains.
- **Ask Hazel** is a chat rail on the right, already scoped to this household. Ask anything; Hazel draws on the profile to answer. It works in both directions: tell Hazel what's changed ("they sold the rental in June"), review the updates Hazel lays out, and confirm. The profile is current.

Next to the household's name you'll also see a small **Data Verification** ring showing how much of the profile you've confirmed. Click it to review new information without leaving the page.

## Your first visit

If Hazel hasn't processed anything for a client yet, the Data tab shows:

> **No household data yet.** "Hazel builds this page from the household's meetings, emails, and uploaded documents. Once a meeting is recorded or an email is synced, the facts Hazel extracts land here — every value with its source."

That's normal. Four ways to change it, in order of speed:

1. **Upload a document.** Drop a statement, tax return, or account document on the **Documents** tab. Facts usually land within minutes.
2. **Tell Hazel.** In Ask Hazel, describe what you already know about the client (accounts, goals, family), review what Hazel proposes to add, and confirm.
3. **Record your next meeting** with the notetaker. Facts appear shortly after your meeting notes are ready.
4. **Connect email**, and facts arrive as clients write to you.

Then spend five minutes in **Data → Review** confirming what Hazel found. See [Managing Client Data](/guides/intelligent-client-profiles/managing-client-data).

## Who can see and change a profile

Profiles follow the same access rules as the rest of a client's record in Hazel:

- **If you can open the client, you can read their profile.** The household Data tab covers every member, so it requires access to the whole household.
- **If you can edit the client, you can maintain their profile**: verify, correct, archive, upload and delete documents.
- **Owners** can see and manage every client's profile, as they can everywhere in Hazel. The Admin role by itself manages firm settings, not client data.
- **There's one shared profile per client**, no private copies. A teammate's verifications and corrections are visible to you, and yours to them, so shared clients get one clean record instead of competing versions. Clients never see their profile; it's internal to your firm.

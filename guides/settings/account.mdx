Manage your personal information, firm details, security, and the compliance language that appears on PDFs Hazel generates for your firm. To open these settings, go to **Settings → Account Settings**.

## PDF disclaimer

The PDF disclaimer is firm-wide compliance language that Hazel automatically appends to every PDF report it generates (for example, exported dashboards and reports from Ask Hazel). It is added in addition to the document-specific disclosure Hazel already includes.

Use it to keep required disclosures consistent across everything your firm produces with Hazel, without having to remember to paste it into individual chats or reports.

### Who can edit it

- **Firm owners** can edit and save the disclaimer.
- **Advisors and other members** see the current disclaimer in read-only form so they know what language will appear on the PDFs they generate. If no disclaimer is set, they see "No firm-wide disclaimer has been set."

Owner-only editing is enforced on the server, so non-owners cannot change the disclaimer even by calling the API directly.

### Fields

The disclaimer has two fields, both optional:

| Field | Limit | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Heading** | 255 characters | Shown in bold above the disclaimer text. Use it for a short label like `Important Disclosures`. |
| **Disclaimer** | 4,000 characters | The body of the disclosure. Plain text; line breaks are preserved. |

Leading and trailing whitespace is trimmed when you save. Clearing both fields removes the disclaimer from new PDFs — existing PDFs are not changed.

### Setting the disclaimer

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Sign in as the firm owner and go to **Settings → Account Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Scroll to **PDF Disclaimer**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Enter an optional **Heading** (for example, `Important Disclosures`).
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Enter the **Disclaimer** body. The counter shows characters remaining out of 4,000.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Click **Save**. The disclaimer is applied to PDFs generated from that point on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To remove the disclaimer, clear both fields and click **Save**.

### Example

```text Heading
Important Disclosures
```

```text Disclaimer
Acme Advisors LLC is a registered investment adviser. Registration does
not imply a certain level of skill or training. This material is for
informational purposes only and does not constitute investment, legal,
or tax advice. Past performance is not indicative of future results.
```

When this is saved, every PDF Hazel generates for your firm ends with the heading in bold followed by the disclaimer body.

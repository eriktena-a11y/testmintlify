# Welcome to Hazel!

You're about two minutes away from having an AI partner that knows your clients, preps your meetings, drafts your emails, and keeps your day organized.

Here's the idea: Hazel pulls together everything scattered across your **CRM, inbox, calendar, and custodial accounts**, and turns it into one clear, conversational view of what matters right now. Ask a question in plain English—_"What should I know before my 2pm with the Garcias?"_—and Hazel answers with the full picture.

This guide walks you through setup and your first few minutes in the app. Let's get you running.

<Info>
  **Were you invited by your firm?** A couple of these steps (choosing a plan and adding payment) are handled by your firm's Owner, so you'll breeze right past them. Everything else is the same.
</Info>

## Setting up Hazel

When you first sign in—with your work email (Google or Microsoft) or Altruist single sign-on—Hazel greets you and walks you through a short setup. You can move **Back** and **Next** at any point, and skip the optional steps.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get started">
    Hazel says hello and lets you know setup takes about two minutes. Click **Get started** to begin.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell Hazel how it can help">
    On **"How can Hazel help you?"**, pick the experience that fits your firm:

    - **Admin AI** — draft emails, summarize documents, answer questions, and tackle daily tasks, all informed by your CRM, custodial data, and notes.
    - **Tax Planning \+ Admin AI** — everything in Admin AI, plus AI-powered tax analysis. Upload 1040s, paystubs, and statements to generate personalized tax strategies.

    Don't overthink it—you can change your plan later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your tools">
    This is where Hazel comes alive. Connect your **email & calendar**, your **CRM**, and (for Altruist firms) your **custodian**. The more you connect, the more Hazel can do. See [Connecting your tools](#connecting-your-tools) below for the details—or click **Skip** and connect later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your plan">
    Choose **Admin AI** or **Admin AI \+ Tax Planning**, and **Monthly** or **Annually** (annual saves the most). You won't be charged until your trial ends, and you can cancel anytime. See current rates on the [pricing page](https://hazel.ai/pricing).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start your free trial">
    Add a payment method to start your **14-day free trial**. You won't be charged until the trial ends—and your card is handled securely.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your team (optional)">
    Bring in your colleagues by email and give each person a role (**Advisor** or **Owner**). You can also turn on the **Tax Planning** add-on per person. Not ready? Skip it—you can invite anyone later from Settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Done!">
    Hazel takes a moment to set up your workspace, then drops you straight into the app. That's it—you're in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Connecting your tools

Connecting your tools is the single most valuable thing you'll do—it's what lets Hazel work from _your_ clients and _your_ day instead of starting from a blank page.

<Info>
  **Your data is safe.** Hazel does not train AI models on your data. When data is shared with AI providers to do work for you, it's covered by strict no-training and data-retention controls, and Hazel is AICPA/SOC certified. [Learn more about Hazel's security](https://hazel.ai/security).
</Info>

### Email & calendar

Connect Google or Microsoft and Hazel can:

- Prep you for every meeting with agendas and the relevant client context.
- Draft replies in your voice, ready for your review.
- Keep your daily priorities current.

You'll be asked to grant permission to see your calendar and to read, compose, and send email on your behalf—that's what powers meeting prep and draft replies. [More on email & calendar →](/guides/integrations/calendar-and-emails/google)

<Note>
  **Signed in with Google or Microsoft SSO?** The first time you log in, Hazel automatically prompts you to connect the calendar for the same account you signed in with — no need to navigate to Settings. The consent screen is pre-filled with your SSO email, so you can approve it in one click.

  If you dismiss the prompt, Hazel won't ask again. You can connect your calendar later from **Settings → Integrations**, but features like meeting prep, agendas, and daily priorities will be limited until you do.
</Note>

### CRM

Your CRM is your client memory—connecting it lets Hazel surface client history in seconds and sync notes and tasks back automatically.

- Click your CRM to connect it. Most use a quick, secure sign-in; **Redtail** asks for your Redtail username and password.
- Hazel imports your contacts, notes, and tasks. The first import can take a little while (you'll see a **"Syncing… may take 10-15 min"** note), then Hazel keeps up automatically.

<Note>
  You can connect **one CRM at a time**. Once you connect one, the others are disabled—your CRM is your single source of truth for client data. [More on CRM integrations →](/guides/integrations/overview)
</Note>

### Custodian (Altruist)

If your firm uses Altruist, connect it to ask Hazel about balances, holdings, recent changes, transactions, and cost basis—answered from the latest account data. You'll review a short **Data Access Consent** screen and click **Agree & Connect**. Access is **read-only** (Hazel can never trade or move money), nothing is stored, and you can disconnect anytime from Settings.

## Your first few minutes in Hazel

When you land in Hazel, you'll see your **Account strength**—a simple progress tracker with one friendly goal: _the more you set up, the more Hazel can do for your firm._ You already get credit for creating your account; here's the rest of the checklist.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your CRM">
    Every client's notes, tasks, and history—right where Hazel needs them. (Skipped it during setup? Connect it here.)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your calendar">
    Walk into every meeting fully prepped—Hazel pulls the client picture for you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Capture your first meeting">
    Hazel can join your meetings automatically and produce notes and follow-ups. Make sure your calendar is connected and **auto-join** is on in [Meeting Settings](/guides/settings/meeting-settings), and Hazel will hop into your next call. [How the notetaker works →](/guides/meeting-assistant/overview/how-it-works)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run a tax analysis">
    If you added Tax Planning, upload a client's tax documents and let Hazel turn them into a clear, interactive summary. [Explore Tax Planning →](/guides/modes/tax-mode)
  </Step>
</Steps>

When you complete the checklist, Hazel celebrates and nudges you to **invite your team**—because Hazel gets better when your whole firm is in it together.

<Tip>
  **Try Ask Hazel right away.** Open a new chat and ask something real:

  - _"Give me a recap of the Thompson household."_
  - _"What follow-ups do I owe clients from this week?"_
  - _"Draft a reply to this email in my voice."_

  [Get the most out of Ask Hazel →](/guides/ask-hazel-chat/best-practices)
</Tip>

## Good to know

- **Your free trial is 14 days.** It starts when the first person at your firm gets going, and you won't be charged until it ends. It rolls into a paid plan automatically unless you cancel beforehand—cancel anytime.
- **Billing is Owner-only.** Only firm Owners choose the plan and manage payment. Invited teammates jump straight to using Hazel.
- **Roles, in one line.** **Owners** run the firm and see everything; **Advisors** work with the clients shared with them; **Assistants** are support seats. [More on roles →](/guides/settings/members/roles-and-permissions)
- **You can skip and come back.** Connecting tools and inviting your team are optional during setup—do them anytime from **Settings**.

<Check>
  Once you're set up, Hazel becomes your daily partner—prepping you for meetings, drafting communications, surfacing client insights, and helping you prioritize your day. Welcome aboard.
</Check>

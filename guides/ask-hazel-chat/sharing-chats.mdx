---
title: "Sharing chats"
---

Share an Ask Hazel chat with colleagues at your firm so they can read the conversation without joining it. This is useful when you want a teammate to review your research, follow along with a client’s history, or hand off context without copying and pasting.

<Info>
  Chat sharing is rolling out gradually behind a feature flag. If you don’t see the **Share** option in your chat header yet, it hasn’t been enabled for your firm — reach out to support if you’d like early access.
</Info>

## Who can share a chat

- **Only the chat’s owner** — the advisor who started the chat — can share it or manage who it’s shared with.
- **Only same-firm colleagues** appear in the share dialog. You can’t share a chat with someone outside your firm.
- Recipients get **view-only** access. They can read every message but can’t send new ones, edit the chat, or re-share it.

## Share a chat

1. Open the chat you want to share.
2. In the chat header, select **Share**.
3. In the **Share chat** dialog, search for a colleague by name or email, then select the checkbox next to each person you want to share with.
4. Select **Share** to grant access. Recipients will see the chat appear in their Ask Hazel history immediately.

Anyone you’ve already shared the chat with appears under **Shared with** at the top of the dialog.

## Revoke access

You can remove access at any time — the chat disappears from the recipient’s history as soon as it’s revoked.

1. Open the chat and select **Share** in the chat header.
2. In the **Share chat** dialog, find the person under **Shared with**.
3. Select the remove icon next to their name to revoke their access.

To revoke everyone at once, select **Unshare all**.

## What recipients see

When a shared chat is opened by someone other than the owner:

- A **View only** banner appears in place of the composer, noting who shared the chat.
- Every message keeps its original attribution, so viewers can tell which turns came from the owner versus Hazel.
- Viewers can read attachments, tool calls, and Hazel’s answers, but they can’t send messages, upload files, or continue the conversation.

If you want a teammate to be able to _act_ on the conversation, ask them to start their own chat — they can reference the details they saw in yours.

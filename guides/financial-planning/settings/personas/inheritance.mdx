You put work into your firm's base template, so you need to know which Client personas will pick it up.

## Follows base, or Customized

<CardGroup cols="2">
  <Card title="Follows base" icon="link">
    No design of its own, so the persona uses your firm's current base design, live. On the **Plan design** card the blueprint is drawn faded, a **Follows base** badge sits beside the heading, and the line under the strictness control reads "This persona follows the base template".
  </Card>

  <Card title="Customized" icon="link-slash">
    Its own design replaces the base, so base-template changes do not reach this persona. The line under the strictness control reads "Customized", and a **Re-sync from base** button appears below it.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Where your firm starts

The three personas your firm arrives with each ship with a plan design of their own, so all three read **Customized** before you touch anything. The left nav of **Settings → Planning** shows it: under **Plan templates**, the subtitle beneath **Base template** reads "No persona follows it right now", and the base pane's **Used by** card is hidden entirely.

A persona follows the base when you create it with **Starting template: Base template**, or when you re-sync one of the three.

<Note>
  A plan for a household that matched no persona uses the base design, and every persona you create from **Base template** starts on it.
</Note>

## What gives a persona its own design

A persona takes its own copy on the **first** change to its design:

- Editing a block on the **Plan design** card: **When it appears**, **Instructions to Hazel**, or the condition for leaving the block out.
- Changing **Strictness** on that card.
- Saving in the layout designer, which you open with **Design layout**. Adding, removing, reordering, and the fields in the settings rail all count.

On the **Plan design** card the change lands as soon as you make it, because Planning settings autosave. A notice appears the first time, reading "This persona now has its own plan design." and "It no longer follows the firm's base design. Re-sync from base any time."

In the layout designer nothing is committed until you save, and that first save asks you first: a dialog says the persona currently follows the firm's base template and that saving gives it a design of its own, with **Keep editing** and **Save design** as the choices. The header chip reads **Follows base template** before that save and **Customized from base** after it.

## What does not follow the base

**Hazel instructions.** A persona's instruction list is its own from the moment the persona exists: creating a persona from **Base template** copies your base instructions across once, and later edits to the base list do not follow. A house instruction you rely on has to appear on each persona.

**Assumptions.** The planning figures live on two firm-wide screens, **Solver assumptions** and **Market assumptions**, and personas inherit them value by value: a persona carries only the values that differ for it, so changing a firm figure reaches every persona that does not set that value. The never-do rules on your base template apply to every plan too, and nothing on a persona loosens them.

## Re-syncing

**Re-sync from base** sits on the persona's **Plan design** card, under the strictness control, and only while the persona reads **Customized**. It puts the persona back on your current base design and keeps it there until the next change. A confirmation dialog headed "Re-sync from base?" comes first.

Re-sync discards rather than merges: anything the persona's design carried that your base does not have goes with it. Instructions and assumption values are untouched, since neither lives in the design.

<Warning>
  Move anything you want to keep into the base template before you re-sync. There is no undo for it.
</Warning>

### When to re-sync

- You improved the base and want this persona on the new shape.
- The persona's design has become a pile of one-off edits rather than a shape you chose.
- The persona took its own design by accident.

Leave it customized while its design is doing real work, or when you want one thing from the base and nothing else: make that change on the persona directly.

## If a base change did not reach your plans

<Steps>
  <Step title="Which persona the plan used">
    Open the plan's **Appendix** and read the **Applied settings** block: it names the persona the plan was built from, or shows none.
  </Step>
  <Step title="That persona's Plan design card">
    If it reads **Customized**, your base change never reached it. Re-sync it, or make the same change on the persona.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Whether the plan predates the change">
    Settings changes apply to plans Hazel builds from then on. A delivered plan keeps the design it was built with until you rebuild it with **Regenerate**, in the top bar of the household's **Financial Planning** tab.
  </Step>
</Steps>

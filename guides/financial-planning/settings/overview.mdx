Decide once how your firm builds plans, so no advisor rebuilds the same choices household by household. **Settings → Planning** sets what a plan contains, in what order, in your words, and against which numbers.

<Note>
  Only firm owners can open this tab. An advisor still has room on one household, adjusting most numbers in that plan's own **Plan settings** panel in the chat view, within the ranges you set. See [Access and availability](/guides/financial-planning/overview/access-and-availability).
</Note>

## The four panes

The left rail groups them under **Plan templates**, **Client persona**, and **Assumptions**.

Two more settings sit on the **Base template** pane, below the instructions list.

**Guardrails.** The rules your firm will not break, however a request is phrased. A persona can add one, never remove one. See [Guardrails](/guides/financial-planning/assumptions/guardrails).

**Client disclaimer.** The footer on every plan, on your screen and in the PDF you hand over. The card opens with wording Hazel has drafted, which is not in force until you edit and save it, so save it once or your plans publish with no footer. **Restore Hazel default** brings the drafted wording back.

## Which layer wins

A plan tags every planning value with the layer that set it, and Hazel ships a default for every value, so anything you have not changed reads as **Firm**. The tags are in the plan's **Appendix**, in its [Applied settings block](/guides/financial-planning/your-data/where-numbers-come-from).

| Layer | What sets it |
| --- | --- |
| **Firm** | The base template, your **Hazel instructions**, your guardrails, and the two assumption editors |
| **Persona** | The matched persona's own instructions, its own plan design if you give it one, and any planning values it wants different from your firm's |
| **This plan** | An advisor's adjustment for one plan, staged in that plan's **Plan settings** panel in the chat view |

A per-plan adjustment belongs to that plan alone, so a second plan for the same household still inherits your firm and persona values. [Solver assumptions](/guides/financial-planning/assumptions/solver-assumptions) covers the panel and the few values it leaves out.

Numbers merge one value at a time, but shape and wording replace: a persona with its own plan design stops following the base design, and its instruction list stands in for the base list rather than adding to it. See [Inheritance and re-syncing](/guides/financial-planning/settings/personas/inheritance).

**A number beats a sentence.** Where an instruction and an assumption disagree about a figure, the value set in **Solver assumptions** or **Market assumptions** wins.

## Where to change what

| You want | Change this |
| --- | --- |
| A section or module in every plan | **Plan design** on the base template |
| Hazel to follow your design more or less closely | **Strictness**, on the Plan design card |
| Every plan to lead with the answer | **Hazel instructions** |
| A different success threshold or reserve length | **Solver assumptions** |
| Different return or inflation figures | **Market assumptions** |
| A change for one plan only | That plan's **Plan settings** panel in the chat view, or ask Hazel in the plan's chat |

<Tip>
  If you ask Hazel for the same change on every plan, that change belongs in settings.
</Tip>

## How saving works

There is no Save button on these panes. An edit saves when you leave the field, so click or tab out of a box before closing the tab, and watch for "Saving…" then "Saved" beside the **Plan templates** heading.

Two exceptions: the **New persona** dialog writes nothing until you select **Create**, and the layout designer, which opens from **Design layout**, has its own **Save changes** button and asks before you leave with work unsaved.

If your planning settings changed somewhere else since your screen loaded, in another tab, in the designer, or by a colleague, Hazel stops saving and shows a red notice asking you to reload. What you typed after that is discarded, so note it down first.

## When a change takes effect

A settings change shapes the next plan Hazel builds, and the next change to a plan that already exists; plans you have already delivered stay as they are.

To move an existing household onto a new shape, open that household's **Financial Planning** tab and select **Regenerate**. That adds a version rather than replacing what your client has seen. See [Versions and regenerating](/guides/financial-planning/the-plan/versions-and-regenerating).

The **Client disclaimer** is the exception. Hazel reads it fresh each time a plan is opened, so editing it also changes the footer on plans you published earlier.

Hazel already holds a lot about your households: statements you uploaded, tax returns, what was said in meetings. The Financial Planning Agent turns that into a financial plan. Hazel reads the household's data, works out what this family's situation calls for, runs the math, and lays out a plan you can present. When you want the plan to say something different, you ask, and Hazel writes a new version.

## What you get

A plan is a document you move around in. It opens on **Summary**, and the left rail walks through **Today**, **Goals**, **Getting there** and **Decisions**, with an **Appendix** at the foot of the list. **Getting there** holds tabs. It opens on **Your priorities**, where Hazel ranks what this household should deal with first, and the other tabs are the topics your firm's template calls for. With Hazel's out-of-the-box setup those are **Cash flow**, **Investments, Estate, Retirement** and **Insurance**.

<CardGroup cols="2">
  <Card title="Built from your data" icon="database">
    Accounts and holdings, tax filings, documents you upload, meetings and transcripts, emails and notes. Nothing to re-type into a form.

    It all reaches Hazel through documents, meetings, emails and notes. Connecting a CRM or a custodian does not by itself put a balance in front of Hazel, so a household you have never met with and hold no paperwork for has nothing to plan from yet.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Sourced, figure by figure" icon="link">
    Sourced figures carry a dotted underline. Select one and Hazel shows where the number came from, the assumptions behind it, and the arithmetic when there is arithmetic to show.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Interactive" icon="sliders">
    Plans carry **levers**: dials such as a retirement age or a savings rate. Move one and the numbers rework on screen while the client watches. A lever appears as a movable control only where Hazel can recompute it in the browser, so not every number behind a plan has a dial. Moving one explores; when you want the change kept, ask Hazel for it.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Shaped by your firm" icon="building">
    Your firm owner sets the shape once: which sections a plan contains, the **Hazel instructions** that steer how it reads, the assumptions to plan against, and the **Guardrails** no plan is allowed to cross.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  Today, a plan belongs to whoever built it. A colleague with full access to the household cannot open a plan you built, and shared financial-plan chats are a fast follow. More on [Access and availability](/guides/financial-planning/overview/access-and-availability).
</Note>

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="You point Hazel at a household">
    Open the household's **Financial Planning** tab, use the **Financial Planning** page, or ask in **Ask Hazel** with the chat in **Financial Planning mode**. The household tab is the one that shows what data Hazel holds and whether the household is ready to build.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hazel gathers and reasons">
    Hazel reads the household's client profile, the accounts and holdings on it, the uploaded documents and the meeting history, then matches the household to one of your firm's **Client personas**, the client types your firm keeps: an early saver, someone close to retirement, someone already retired. The matched persona shapes which sections this plan gets, how it should read, and some of the assumptions it starts from. When nothing fits, Hazel builds on your firm's **Base template** alone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hazel runs the math and writes the plan">
    The numbers come out of a library of planning calculations, run with your firm's **Solver assumptions**, the planning choices behind a plan such as the success threshold a plan has to clear and how many years of spending to hold in reserve, and your firm's **Market assumptions**, the return, volatility, inflation and tax figures to plan against.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You review, tune and present">
    Open **Plan orientation**, the info button at the top of the plan, for a short read on what this plan concludes and why. Then check a few figures against their sources, move a lever or two, and edit any sentence in place. To ask Hazel for a change, you need the plan's conversation: in the chat view that is the **Edit** button, and on the household's Financial Planning tab it is **Open chat**. When the plan reads the way you want, download the saved plan as a PDF for the meeting.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  A figure in a plan comes either from something Hazel read or from an assumption, and the plan labels which. Where something material is missing, Hazel asks you before drafting. On the household's **Financial Planning** tab, a question you leave alone settles into a reasonable assumption so an unattended build still finishes. A build you started in a chat waits for you instead. Either way, read the figures resting on an assumption before you present.
</Note>

New to Hazel's Financial Planning Agent? Keep the [Glossary](/guides/financial-planning/glossary) open in a second tab. Persona, lever, solver assumption and the rest of the vocabulary on this page get a line each.

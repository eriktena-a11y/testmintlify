You want every plan your firm delivers to carry your firm's shape, not a default. Plan design sets that shape once, and Hazel fills it with each household's analysis.

<Note>
  Nothing here touches a plan you have already delivered. A design shapes the plans Hazel builds from now on, and an existing plan stays until someone rebuilds it.
</Note>

Find it in **Settings → Planning**, on the **Base template** every plan the firm builds starts from, and on each persona under **Client persona**. Planning settings belong to the firm owner, so ask whoever owns your firm's Hazel account if you cannot see the section.

## Reading the blueprint

The card headed **Plan layout** draws your whole design at once.

| What you see | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Bands** | Top-level sections, which become the plan's main navigation. A band without tabs shows its module count. |
| **Indented sub-bands** | Child sections, which become tabs inside the parent, whose row says "Shown as tabs". |
| **Chips** | Blocks. One chip is one piece of the plan: writing, a chart, a table, key figures, levers. |
| **Markers** | When each piece appears, under the legend headed **When it appears**: a solid check for **Always**, in every plan; a dashed circle for **When it applies**, only where it fits the household. |

A chip carrying a module shows the module's name, sometimes with a badge: "2 levers" for the what-if controls that piece offers, or one starting with "Calc" for the calculation behind its figures.

A section holding no pieces reads "Hazel composes this section freely". Under the drawing, Hazel counts your sections, the planning modules in play, and the pieces set to Always. **See what's left out** opens the module library on the rest.

### Strictness, and whose design this is

**Strictness**, in the card's header, sets how closely Hazel follows the blueprint: **Exact**, **Guided** or **Flexible**, and new firms start on **Flexible**. The same panel holds one switch, **Allow additions** ("Hazel may add sections or blocks you didn't design"), which Exact locks off. [Strictness](/guides/financial-planning/settings/personas/strictness) covers what each mode does and how it plays against each piece's own setting.

Before anyone changes it, the base template wears a **Hazel default** badge. A persona nobody has changed wears **Follows base**, faded, tracking the base template live. Your first edit gives that persona a copy of its own, and **Re-sync from base** hands it back whenever you want.

Every chip can be moved, removed or reworded, including the ones Hazel put there. A design keeps at least one section, and removing a section or tab takes everything inside it.

## The two ways to change it

<CardGroup cols="2">
  <Card title="Select a chip" icon="hand-pointer">
    A panel opens with three settings for that chip: **When it appears**, **Instructions to Hazel**, and **Leave out when**, the one condition it may be dropped. Press **Done** when finished. Use it to shape how a piece is written, without leaving the settings page.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Select Design layout" icon="table-columns" href="/guides/financial-planning/plan-design/using-the-editor">
    Opens the full editor, for changing what is there: add and remove modules, reorder blocks, restructure sections and tabs, pin exact headings, and preview the shape filled in with a made-up household.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  Planning settings have no Save button. What you type saves a moment after you click away, and a quiet **Saved** appears at the top of the page. The full editor is the exception: changes there wait for its own save button.
</Tip>

## Design, instructions and assumptions

- **Plan design**: what a plan contains, and where. Binds as tightly as your strictness setting.
- **Instructions**: how it is written, and what to emphasize. Guidance Hazel weighs.
- **Assumptions**: the numbers behind every figure. Used as given, and a persona can override a firm value while one plan can override both.

Making a topic appear in every plan takes two settings: the piece on **Always**, and strictness on **Guided** or **Exact**. A module you add starts on **When it applies** unless you use the add option that always includes it.

## Where the default shape comes from

Out of the box a plan navigates **Summary** (its own heading reads "Executive summary"), **Today**, **Goals**, **Getting there** and **Decisions**. Getting there opens on Your priorities, then three tabs your modules put there: Cash flow, Retirement and Insurance. Investments and Estate plan wait in the module library.

That shape comes from eight modules: Net worth & balance sheet, Emergency fund, Goal funding, Insurance, Cash flow, Debt paydown, Retirement, and Monte Carlo success.

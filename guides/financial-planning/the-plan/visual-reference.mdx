You know the point you want a plan to make, so you need the block that makes it and what it needs to work. Every one below can go in your firm's layout in [Plan design](/guides/financial-planning/plan-design/overview), or be asked for by name in chat.

In the layout editor they sit in the collapsed **Building blocks** shelf at the bottom of the **Modules** tab, ready to drag into a section, grouped there by kind: Narrative, Numbers & highlights, Charts, Tables, Interactive, Groups.

<Note>
  **Priority list** belongs in the **Your priorities** tab of Getting there, and **Probability bar** cannot go in the **Goals** section. Everything else goes wherever the plan needs it.
</Note>

Once in a while Hazel builds a one-off visual that is not in this list. It carries a **Generative** badge on the plan, and you cannot place it in a layout.

## Words

| Block | What it says | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Narrative** | A few sentences in plain language, written for this household | Nothing beyond the plan's content |
| **Mission statement** | The household's own words about what the money is for | Something the household has said about it |

## Headline numbers

| Block | What it says | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Key numbers** | A row of headline figures | The few figures that matter most |
| **Highlight cards** | What's working, what needs attention | Short points to group |
| **Info cards** | A card per item: goals, accounts, opportunities, or a checklist | Comparable items |

## Probability

| Block | What it says | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Success gauge** | Probability of success today, next to probability with the plan | Two modeled results |
| **Probability bar** | Today's probability against where the plan lands, with an optional target marker | Both probabilities |
| **Range of outcomes** | The spread of results across simulated market paths | Results at two or more points in time. The band reads as a spread only while **Monte Carlo** is on in [Market assumptions](/guides/financial-planning/assumptions/market-assumptions), where it starts on |

## Time

| Block | What it says | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Scenario paths** | Wealth over time: the current path against the planned one | One to four paths, and a title naming what the lines cover (portfolio balance, not net worth) |
| **Stacked columns** | Yearly columns split into their sources | A value per source per year, on one shared axis |
| **Inflows & outflows** | Money in and money out, side by side over the years | One flow above the zero line, one below, a figure per year |
| **Whole-life cash flow** | Every year's money in, out, and remaining, as a chart plus a table | A solved year-by-year ledger, or up to three to switch between |
| **Time spans** | Commitments side by side over the years, the area showing what each costs | Two or three items, each with a start, an end, and a recurring amount |
| **Timeline** | Steps in order across the years | Events, each with the year or window it lands in |

## Composition

| Block | What it says | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Donut chart** | How a total splits across its pieces | One or more slices |
| **Breakdown chart** | One total, broken into its parts | A stated total, parts that add up to it, nothing negative |
| **Mix comparison** | Labeled strips showing how each mix splits | One mix, or several to compare (current against target) |

## Comparison

| Block | What it says | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Before & after** | Up to five key figures, before and after the recommendations | Dials with a current and a recommended setting, both read from the plan's own model, never typed in. To change one, ask Hazel in the plan's chat |
| **Bar chart** | Bars comparing amounts across years or categories | A value per category |
| **Scenario comparison** | Two or three choices side by side, with a verdict | Two or three options, and rows whose figures genuinely differ |
| **Progress to target** | Stacked pieces against a target, with the space above them named | A target, the pieces against it, and a meaning for the space above: a gap, room to spare, or money accounted for |
| **Change table** | Rows shifting from one value to another, with a total | Each row's new value, plus the old one where there is one |

## Ranking and priority

| Block | What it says | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ranked list** | The Social Security claiming decision: the monthly payment at each start age, plus the lifetime total | Two to six start ages, today's option as the reference, and a planning-age dial. For other rankings, ask for a **Bar chart** |
| **Priority list** | Hazel's ranked priorities for this household, one panel per priority | Leave it empty and Hazel picks the priorities; pin blocks inside to require specific ones |
| **Savings order** | Where each next dollar of savings goes, in order | The rungs in order, each with an annual and a monthly figure |

## Decisions and actions

| Block | What it says | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Recommendation** | One recommendation, its reasoning, and Approve or Ignore. Approving records it in the Decision log | A decision worth putting in front of the household |
| **Decision log** | Decisions made, approved, and still open | Nothing. It starts empty and fills as decisions are approved, so place it early |
| **Questions** | Up to three assumptions for the household to confirm or correct, pre-filled with what Hazel assumed | At least one assumption Hazel wants confirmed. The plan already stands on those values, so answering is optional |
| **Asset location moves** | Which holdings move to which accounts, and why | An account to move from, one to move to, and a reason |

## Structure and interaction

| Block | What it says | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Tab group** | Each block inside becomes a tab, and Hazel finalizes the grouping when it builds | The blocks you want behind tabs |
| **Levers to try** | Dials the household can move, with every bound figure moving as the dial moves | One or more dials the plan defines. Only dials your browser can recompute appear as controls; ask in chat for anything needing Hazel to re-solve the plan |
| **Table** | Rows and columns: accounts, amounts, schedules | Column headings and at least one row |
